Affective 
1. To strengthen project management and self-learning skills with a positive attitude while executing the project activities.
2. To demonstrate the completed project with clarity and be able to communicate professionally with the audience.
3. To expand the interim report into a complete final report with critical evaluation and performance analysis using a systematic approach to implementing solutions for the 
project with technical contributions and entrepreneurial skills.
4. To determine the technical development tasks, critical evaluations, performance analysis and data representation for effective project solution implementation.
Justification for including the course in the programme:
The final year project serves as the culminating experience of a degree program. Students will expand their project development in accordance with the plan defined in Project 
1 and engage in technical programming, problem-solving, troubleshooting, system/module integration, implementation, testing, performance analysis, conducting experiments, 
and result evaluation. Students are also required to communicate regularly with their respective supervisors to report the progress of their projects. Through this course, 
students will acquire knowledge and skills in research or application development methodologies related to the project title and prepare a complete final report. At the end of 
the trimester, students must submit a complete final report and present their project findings and results to the supervisor and panel.
